BEFORE YOU BOOK

Frequently
asked questions.

Do I need an appointment?+

Appointments are recommended, especially for bridal and occasion services, so enough time can be reserved for your look.

Can I enquire for a bridal package?+

Yes. Use any Bridal Enquiry button and include your wedding or function date, venue and required services.

Do you provide saree draping and hair styling?+

Yes. Saree draping and hair styling can be requested individually or along with occasion and bridal makeup.

How do I confirm price and availability?+

Send an enquiry with the service and preferred date. Samrithaa's can then confirm availability and the applicable service pricing.
